Asociación Cultural Saged is a non-profit cultural association constituted in Valencia, Spain, for the promotion and development of intercultural dialogue, creative education, and community-building through art, ceramics, music, and cultural programming. It provides infrastructure — physical space, legal structure, and institutional capacity — for the cultural initiatives of its members and the communities it serves.

Mission

"To build and maintain a shared creative space — physical and social — where people of different cultures, languages, and backgrounds make things together and, through that act, integrate."

Valencia is home to one of the largest Russian and Ukrainian-speaking communities in Spain. Asociación Cultural Saged was founded to provide this community — and the broader intercultural population of the city — with a stable, professionally run cultural infrastructure.

The association holds a physical studio in Ciutat Vella and operates a programme of ceramics classes, music education, children's creative workshops, and community events in Russian, Ukrainian, English, and Spanish.

It was constituted as a cultural association to formalise this work: to make it fundable, scalable, and institutionally legible — while keeping it rooted in the direct relationships and creative practices that make it work.

Founding purposes · Art. 4, Estatutos

Six statutory
purposes.

01
Community and cultural space

To promote and develop a community and cultural space around art, culture, creativity, and collective cultural initiatives.

02
Support for member initiatives

To support the creative, cultural, and educational initiatives of community members, facilitating the conditions for their development.

03
Intercultural exchange

To create an environment of cultural exchange, dialogue, and cooperation between people of different countries, cultures, and professions.

04
Education and knowledge exchange

To develop cultural and educational programmes for the exchange of knowledge and creative practices.

05
Social integration

To foster social integration, intercultural understanding, and active participation in the cultural life of the community.

06
Cultural development

To promote the creation and development of cultural projects, initiatives, and spaces that enrich cultural life.

Current cultural
programme.

The following recurring programmes are operated by the association at its studio in Ciutat Vella, Valencia. All programmes are led by qualified instructors. Languages noted per programme.

Ceramics · Adults
Ceramics with Korytsia

Hand-building ceramic classes for adults led by Korincia Gasikóvska (Korytsia), a Ukrainian ceramic artist based in Valencia. Runs as a two-session mini-course (€60) or single drop-in session (€30). Maximum 6 participants per session. Every Thursday, three time slots.

Story Therapy

A clay-based creative programme for children in which participants build characters and miniature settings inspired by fairy tales and stories. Designed around imaginative play and hands-on process. Led by Korytsia. Ages 5 and up.
