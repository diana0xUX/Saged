Asociación Cultural · Ciutat Vella, Valencia

A cultural home,
built by its members.

Saged brings the craft, music and thinking of Eastern Europe and the Baltics into conversation with Valencia. We are makers, engineers, designers and teachers who organise locally, share what we know, and rise together.

Our values

What we believe

Made by hand

Everything here starts with making something real, side by side at the same table.

Ceramics · Adults
Ceramics with Korytsia

Hand-building ceramic classes for adults led by Korincia Gasikóvska (Korytsia), a Ukrainian ceramic artist based in Valencia. Runs as a two-session mini-course (€60) or single drop-in session (€30). Maximum 6 participants per session. Every Thursday, three time slots.

Ceramics with Diana

Beginner and evening ceramics sessions led by Diana Voroneca. Structured for participants with no prior experience. Runs Tuesday, Wednesday, and Friday. Classes conducted in English and Spanish.

Clay therapy · Children ages 5+
Story Therapy

A clay-based creative programme for children in which participants build characters and miniature settings inspired by fairy tales and stories. Designed around imaginative play and hands-on process. Led by Korytsia. Ages 5 and up.

Music education
Handpan School

Group handpan music lessons for beginners. Launched October 2026. One session per week. The handpan is a steel tongue instrument well-suited to contemplative, community-oriented music practice.

Participants

No membership is required to attend classes and events. The studio is open to anyone. Contact us directly to book a session or enquire about the schedule.
