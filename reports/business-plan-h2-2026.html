Revenue Streams — Targets

Core · Classes

Adult ceramics (Korytsia + Vita)

Korytsia runs 3 Thu slots × 6 people × €60. Vita runs Wed + Fri sessions at ~€40 per person. Studio keeps 50% of each instructor's revenue.

Now: ~€600–800/month studio share
Target by Nov: €1,500/month (fuller slots, Vita established)
Growth · Coworking

Studio residents

5 spots available at €200/month each. Currently 1 resident (Katya). Each additional resident is pure margin — zero extra cost once signed.

Now: €200/month (1 resident)
Target by Oct: €600/month (3 residents)
Growth · Events

Private events

Birthday parties, hen nights, corporate team events. One private booking fills a slot that might otherwise have 2–3 people in it, at 3–5× the revenue per hour. No ads needed — referral and Instagram are enough.

Now: occasional, unpriced
Target by Sep: 1–2 events/month at €350 avg = €700
Core · Kids

Kids classes

€25/child, groups of 4–6. Already validated — trial ran in June. Low ad cost because the audience finds via word of mouth among parents. Weekends with Korytsia.

Now: trial mode, no fixed schedule
Target by Sep: weekly slot, 5 kids = €500/month gross
Medium term · Online

Digital masterclasses

Recorded ceramic technique classes sold via Stripe. No studio space required. 5 sales already in June. A structured course at €50 sold to 30 students = €1,500 one-time, zero marginal cost after production.

Now: informal, 5 Stripe sales Jun
Target by Nov: one packaged course live, €500+/month
Holiday · Dec

Gift vouchers + seasonal workshops

December is the highest-leverage month. Ceramic class gift vouchers, "make your own Christmas gifts" workshops, corporate gifting. Booked via Instagram story + DM — no landing page needed.

Now: not yet launched
Target Dec: €1,000+ from events + vouchers alone

What Not to Do

Scale Meta ads before knowing source June proved more spend ≠ more bookings. Understand what drove July first.
Add a 3rd instructor or major product line Two instructors + coworking + events is enough to hit targets. Adding more complexity before the model is stable creates risk, not growth.
Merge RU + UA ads or audiences Non-negotiable. Two communities, two campaigns always.
Run paid ads for cultural events Events convert organically. Paid ads for events wasted money in H1.
Wait for "perfect" before launching private events One Instagram story with a date + price is enough. Start August.
