Net Result — Projected vs Actual

The trajectory was right, the magnitude was off. The June forecast correctly called the direction — losses narrowing each month toward break-even. But July undershot (−€182 vs −€76) and August only crossed zero because costs fell more than expected, not because income hit the target. Break-even is real but fragile.
