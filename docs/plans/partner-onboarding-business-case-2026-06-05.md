## TL;DR

**Single-studio ceiling**: ~€90-100K/year gross at full capacity; **€30-40K/year net profit after tax**. Real, achievable in 12-18 months with the right team and partnership structure.

## Spanish taxes — what each path actually costs

### Path 1 — Autónomo (self-employed, simplest)

**Best for**: Diana sole owner, husband as informal contributor, profit under €40K/yr.

**Costs:**
- **Cuota autónomo (social security)**: €230-290/month for tier 1, rising with income. ~€3,000/yr.
- **IRPF (income tax)**: progressive, 19% (first €12,450) → 24% → 30% → 37% → 45% → 47%. On €25K profit ≈ €4,200 IRPF. On €45K profit ≈ €10,500.
- **IVA (VAT)**: 21% on adult classes + coworking. Quarterly returns. (Kids classes may qualify for educational exemption — check with gestor.)
- **Retención IRPF**: 15% withheld on invoices to other Spanish businesses.

**Husband joining as autónomo**:
- He becomes second autónomo (another €3K/yr cuota)
- They invoice each other (administrative pain)
- OR he becomes *autónomo colaborador* (family member helping) — reduced cuota €100/mo, must be 1st-degree family, max 50% share of profit
- *Family collaborator is the cheapest path if husband stays low-paid contributor*

### Path 2 — Sociedad Limitada (SL, real company)

**Best for**: Real partnership, husband bringing capital, profit above €40K/yr, plans to scale.

**Setup costs:**
- Min capital: **€3,000** (€1 since 2022 reform but practically €3K)
- Notary + registry: €500-800
- Gestor for formation: €500-1,000
- Total setup: ~€1,500-2,500 one-time

**Ongoing taxes:**
- **Corporate tax**: 25% on profit. 23% for newly-created SLs first 2 years if profit <€1M (NEW SL only — Saged would qualify if registered fresh).
- **No IVA difference** vs autónomo (still 21% on most revenue).
- **Owner pays self via**: salary (full SS + IRPF withholding, like employee) OR dividends (taxed at 19-28% personal capital gains). Optimal mix: pay yourself ~€1,200-1,500/mo salary + take dividends on remainder.

**Cap table options** (assume 100 shares):
- **50/50**: equal partnership. Cleanest for marriage; both equal voice.
- **51/49 (Diana)**: she keeps tie-breaker. Common for founder-spouse setups.
- **60/40 (Diana)**: she founded it. Recognizes pre-marriage build.
- **70/30 (Diana)**: husband as junior partner contributing capital, not founder credit.

> **Spanish marriage regime warning**: Most of Spain defaults to *régimen de gananciales* — assets earned during marriage are jointly owned regardless of who's on the deed. If you're under gananciales, the equity split on paper may not match real legal ownership. **Confirm your marriage regime before signing anything.** Catalonia + Balearics default is separate property (separación de bienes). Valencia (Comunidad Valenciana) historically had its own foral regime but defaults to gananciales since 2016.

### Path 3 — SLNE (Sociedad Limitada Nueva Empresa)

Like SL but faster setup, fewer shareholders allowed. Less common now. **Skip — SL is more flexible.**

### Tax comparison at three profit levels

| Annual profit | Autónomo (1 owner) | Autónomo (Diana + husband colaborador) | SL (25%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| €25,000 | ~€7,200 tax + SS = €17,800 take-home | ~€8,500 combined SS + IRPF = €16,500 take-home | ~€7,000 corp + €1,500 dividend = €16,500 take-home |
| €45,000 | ~€13,500 tax + SS = €31,500 take-home | ~€14,000 combined = €31,000 take-home | ~€11,250 corp + €3K div = €30,700 take-home |
| €100,000 | ~€35,000 tax + SS = €65,000 take-home | ~€34,000 combined = €66,000 take-home | ~€25,000 corp + €8K div = €67,000 take-home |

**Crossover**: SL becomes meaningfully better above ~€60K profit AND when you want to retain earnings inside the company for expansion. Below €40K, autónomo wins on simplicity.

## Section 4 — Decision framework for the husband conversation

### Three honest questions to answer together

**1. What is he actually bringing — capital, time, both?**

| Contribution | Suggested partnership structure |
|---|---|
| Capital only (€10-30K) | Silent partner / investor. 20-40% equity in SL. He doesn't work day-to-day. |
| Time only (full-time) | Co-founder / co-operator. 40-50% equity. Becomes salaried administrator in SL. |
| Both (€15K + 20 hrs/wk) | True co-founder. 50/50 in SL. |
| Both (€30K+ + full-time) | Senior partner. 50/50 with veto rights on capital decisions. |

**2. What does each of you do day-to-day?**

The studio needs:
- **Sales + marketing + operations** (Diana's current role) — 25-30 hrs/wk
- **Teaching delivery** (Korytsia + Vita) — 15-20 hrs/wk paid by output
- **Finance + admin + legal** — 5-10 hrs/wk (currently Diana ad-hoc)
- **Business development** (partnerships, B2B, press, corporate) — 10-15 hrs/wk (currently zero)
- **Studio physical maintenance** (kiln, supplies, cleaning) — 3-5 hrs/wk

**Husband's best entry slot**: Business development + finance/admin. Frees Diana to focus on customer experience + creative + the parts she's actually best at.

**3. What's the timeline + exit clause?**

Partnership agreements without exit clauses become hostage situations. Even between spouses. Especially between spouses.

Build into the agreement:
- **Vesting**: equity earned over 24-36 months (protects against early exit)
- **Buyout formula**: how does someone exit at year 3? Multiple of trailing 12-mo profit? Asset value? Pre-agreed.
- **Tiebreaker for 50/50 splits**: who decides? Coin flip? External advisor? (50/50 with no tiebreaker = paralysis.)
- **Marriage clause**: what happens if marriage ends? Most ugly. Most necessary. Spouse-founder partnerships should have this written.

### Recommended path (opinion, not gospel)

If both partners commit:

1. **Form a new SL** (clean slate, simpler than transferring autónomo into SL later)
2. **Register trademark "Saged.club"** before equity discussion (asset clarity)
3. **Diana contributes**: domain, brand, website, customer list, Korytsia relationship → valued at ~€40K equity-equivalent
4. **Husband contributes**: capital (€10-20K) + time → valued at ~€30-40K equity-equivalent
5. **Resulting split**: 50/50 or 55/45 Diana
6. **Min capital injection**: €3K each = €6K starting cash
7. **Owner salaries**: €1,200/mo each starting; ramp with profit
8. **Use a gestor for SL formation** (€500-1,000) — do NOT do this without one

### Red flags to watch in the conversation

- **"We don't need a written agreement, we're married"** — biggest failure pattern. Get it in writing precisely *because* you're married. Avoids a future fight you can't predict.
- **"I'll just inject money for now and we'll figure it out"** — leads to ambiguity about whether it was a loan, a gift, or equity. Decide upfront, document.
- **"Let's split everything 50/50, easy"** — only works with a tiebreaker mechanism. Otherwise becomes deadlock the first time you disagree on a hire.
- **"My contribution is so much harder to quantify"** — both sides feel this. The agreement should pre-empt this by codifying roles + decision rights, not by trying to make everything quantifiable.
