## 1. Certificado de Delitos de Naturaleza Sexual — for Diana, Korytsia, Vita

**Why most critical:** Mandatory under Ley Orgánica 8/2021 for ANY person in habitual contact with minors. No exceptions for autónomos, contractors, profit-share collaborators. Without it, hosting a single kids birthday party = legal exposure + reputation risk if challenged. The cheapest, fastest item on this list — and the most asymmetric (huge downside if missing, zero downside if obtained).

**Process:**
- Apply online at sede.mjusticia.gob.es (needs Cl@ve / digital certificate / DNIe).
- Or in person at any Oficina de Justicia with prior cita previa.
- Or by ordinary mail with form.

**Time:** Instant online (PDF download) / 1-2 weeks in person or by mail.

**Cost:** **Free.** All three certificates.

## 2. Declaración Responsable de Apertura (Activity declaration) — Ayuntamiento de Valencia

**Why critical:** Without this, the city can order immediate closure. The "Declaración Responsable" is the faster route for "actividades inocuas" (harmless activities); a ceramic teaching workshop with kiln *might* qualify, *might* require a fuller "Licencia Ambiental" depending on classification of kiln + storage + capacity.

**Process:**
- Submit a technical project ("proyecto técnico") prepared by a competent technician (arquitecto técnico or ingeniero), describing the premises, the activity, fire safety, accessibility, kiln specifications.
- Submit "Declaración Responsable de Apertura" at sede.valencia.es (procedure code LA.AC.85 or LA.AC.90).
- After 1 month with no inspection or with conformity report, you can operate under your own responsibility.

**Time:** 1-3 months total (mostly project preparation).

**Cost:**
- Technical project by arquitecto técnico: **€350–€800 + VAT** (basic "harmless activity" range in Valencia).
- Municipal fees ("tasas") for the declaración: **€100–€400** depending on premises surface.
- **Total estimate: €450–€1,200.**

**Action:**
- [ ] Contact 2-3 arquitectos técnicos in Valencia for quotes (mention "actividad inocua" + ceramic teaching + Ciutat Vella + small studio).
- [ ] Confirm whether the kiln triggers "Licencia Ambiental" (heavier process) instead of "Declaración Responsable" (lighter). The technician will tell you.
- [ ] Submit at sede.valencia.es and start the 1-month clock.

## 3. Seguro de Responsabilidad Civil (Civil Liability Insurance)

**Why critical:** Covers third-party injury or damage claims. A kid burns hand on warm clay, an adult trips and hurts themselves, a parent's bag is damaged — without this, you pay out of pocket. One serious claim could bankrupt the studio. The most asymmetric financial protection you can buy.

**Process:**
- Get quotes from 3 insurers: Mapfre, AXA, Allianz, Zurich — or smaller specialists via comparador (Acierto, Rastreator).
- Make sure the policy explicitly covers: teaching activity, minors on premises, fire from kiln, electrical equipment.
- Confirm coverage applies during co-hosted classes (Vita ramp-up phase) and external contractor activity (Korytsia).

**Cost:** **€200–€500/year** for small workshop with minors. Single-instructor, single-location, low employee count, moderate-risk activity → mid-range.

**Action:**
- [ ] Request 3 quotes; specify "taller de cerámica con clases para menores."
- [ ] Compare coverages (not just price); minimum coverage €300,000 per claim recommended.
- [ ] Activate before the first paying class with new audience (Vita's birthday party).

## 4. Update Modelo 036 / IAE epígrafe to include ceramic teaching activity

**Why critical:** You are autónoma but the registered epígrafe may not cover ceramic teaching. Without the correct IAE code, every Stripe receipt and every contractor factura is technically misfiled. Risk: a hacienda inspection finds undeclared activity, retroactive penalties.

**Process:**
- Verify your current epígrafe on your última copia of Modelo 036 / 037.
- For ceramic teaching workshops, the candidates are:
  - **Epígrafe 826** — "Personal docente de enseñanzas diversas, tales como educación física y deportes, idiomas, mecanografía, preparación de exámenes y oposiciones y similares" — for the teaching activity.
  - **Epígrafe 933.9** — "Otras actividades de enseñanza, tales como idiomas, corte y confección, mecanografía, taquigrafía, preparación de exámenes y oposiciones" — for a teaching center/local.
  - **Epígrafe 861** — for own production of ceramic artwork.
- File Modelo 036 modification (declaración censal de modificación) online via Agencia Tributaria portal with your digital certificate.

**Cost:** **Free** (filing only). If gestoría handles: **€30–€80**.

**Action:**
- [ ] Confirm current epígrafe with your gestor.
- [ ] If missing, add 933.9 (teaching center) — and 861 if you sell finished pieces.
- [ ] Update before sending the next Stripe receipt for ceramic class revenue.

## 5. Seguro Multirriesgo (Premises & Contents Insurance)

**Why critical:** Covers fire, theft, water damage, electrical damage to the studio + kiln + tools. The kiln alone is €2,000–€4,000 replacement. Without this, one electrical fire = studio gone. Less urgent than RC because no third-party harm, but high financial exposure.

**Process:**
- Often bundled with Responsabilidad Civil from the same insurer (cheaper combined).
- Make sure kiln is listed as covered equipment (some policies exclude "industrial kilns" or limit coverage).
- Cover contents up to ~€10,000–€20,000 starting estimate.

**Cost:** **€300–€600/year** for small premises with low-value contents but kiln. Combined RC + Multirriesgo: ~€500–€900/year.

**Action:**
- [ ] When requesting RC quotes (item 3), also request bundled multirriesgo.
- [ ] Confirm kiln coverage specifically.
- [ ] Document kiln serial + value with insurer at policy start.

## 6. Written contracts with Korytsia and Vita

**Why critical:** The 50/50 profit-share is currently verbal. Without written contracts: (a) no proof of arrangement if either party disputes; (b) tax-side: Hacienda expects contractors to issue facturas — if they don't, the income/expense classification is wrong; (c) Korytsia and Vita each need to be autónomas themselves to issue facturas legally; if not, the relationship may be reclassified as disguised employment ("falso autónomo") which has heavy penalties.

**Process:**
- Use a simple "contrato de colaboración mercantil" (mercantile collaboration contract) template — define: scope, profit split base (gross vs net), termination terms, IP ownership of works, confidentiality.
- Each contractor must be registered as autónoma OR you must structure a different legal relationship (employment, which is heavier).
- Optional: have a gestor or abogado review one template; reuse for both.

**Cost:**
- DIY from a template: **Free**.
- Abogado/gestor review of template: **€100–€300 one-time** (reusable for future contractors).
- Korytsia & Vita's own autónoma registration (if not done): each pays their own ~€200-300/quarter (cuota autónomos).

**Action:**

## 7. Sign authorization (cartel/rótulo) — Ciutat Vella BIC zone

**Why critical:** Hanging an unauthorized sign in a BIC zone can trigger heritage-violation fines and forced removal. Ciutat Vella is under Ley de Patrimonio Cultural Valenciano 4/1998 + Special Protection Plan. The fines aren't ruinous, but mandatory removal mid-campaign is worse than the delay.

**Process:**
- Determine whether your building is itself BIC-listed, BRL (Bien de Relevancia Local), or just in the protected ambit.
- For "rótulo en banderola" (flag sign): max 1m projection, max 1/3 building height, max 0.30m depth.
- For "rótulo en fachada" (wall-mounted): more permissive but still needs declaración + technical report ("informe técnico").
- Submit at sede.valencia.es procedure VP.PU.55 ("Licencia para la instalación de publicidad").
- If building is BIC-listed: heritage report ("informe de patrimonio") also required from Consellería de Cultura.

**Time:** 1-3 months for approval if heritage report needed; weeks if simpler declaración.

**Cost:**
- Technical report / project for sign: **€150–€400**.
- Municipal fees: **€50–€200**.
- Sign manufacture: **€100–€500** depending on size and material.
- Heritage report (if BIC building): adds **€200–€500** + longer wait.
- **Total estimate: €300–€1,500** depending on complexity.

**Action:**
- [ ] Find out: is C/ de les Cuines, 8 itself BIC-listed, or just in the BIC ambit? (Search at cultura.gva.es BIC registry.)
- [ ] Don't hang the sign before authorization comes through. Plan a temporary cardboard / vinyl-window version for the transition.
- [ ] Request quotes from local rotulistas + ask them which permit they handle.

## 9. Prevención de Riesgos Laborales (PRL) — workplace risk assessment

**Why critical:** Required even for solo autónomos with workplace (more strict if you have employees, but contractor + minors on premises means you must have at least a basic evaluación de riesgos).

**Process:**
- Contract a Servicio de Prevención Ajeno (external prevention service) — common providers: Quirón Prevención, Cualtis, Fraternidad-Muprespa.
- They produce: evaluación de riesgos del puesto, plan de prevención, plan de emergencia.
- For tiny operations, "trabajador designado" (yourself, with a basic course) is possible but the external route is simpler.

**Cost:**
- External prevention service: **€100–€300/year**.
- DIY "trabajador designado" course: **€50–€150 one-time** + your time.

**Action:**
- [ ] Get 2 quotes from prevention services.
- [ ] Activate before first kids class with multiple parents+kids on premises.

## 10. GDPR / Protección de Datos (especially for minors' data)

**Why critical:** You collect WhatsApp numbers, names, kid ages, payment data. Spain enforces RGPD + LOPDGDD. Special category: data of minors requires parental consent. AEPD (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) sanctions can be severe but enforcement against tiny businesses is rare unless someone complains.

**Process:**
- Website already has consent banner (Meta Pixel). Good.
- Add a **Registro de Actividades de Tratamiento (RAT)** — internal doc listing what data you collect, why, where stored, retention period. Required by law even for tiny businesses.
- Add **privacy policy** to website (if missing) covering: data collected, purpose, retention, rights (acceso, rectificación, supresión, portabilidad).
- For kids: explicit parental consent on intake form ("Consiento al tratamiento de los datos de mi hijo/a para gestión de la clase y comunicación").
- Designate Diana as **Responsable del Tratamiento** in the RAT.

**Cost:**
- DIY using AEPD templates (free at aepd.es): **Free**.
- Gestor/abogado GDPR audit + RAT: **€150–€500 one-time**.

**Action:**
- [ ] Check current saged.club privacy policy completeness — does it list kids data?
- [ ] Add explicit parental consent line to WhatsApp intake template (already partial — formalize).
- [ ] Create RAT document in `finances/` or similar gitignored location.
