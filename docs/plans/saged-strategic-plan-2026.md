# Saged — Strategic Plan 2026
_Prepared July 2026. Internal use only._

---

## Vision

Saged is a prestige ceramics brand with a curated artist roster — not a casual drop-in studio. Growth runs through relationships and word of mouth, not advertising. Community cool over marketing polish.

Two interconnected parts:
- **Urban studio** (Ciutat Vella, Valencia) — community building, skill progression, prestige
- **Finca retreat venue** (Carcaixent, ~4.5 ha citrus grove) — ceramic retreats, venue rental, agrotourism

The studio feeds the finca: regulars become retreat guests. Neither works as well without the other.

**Primary audience:** Russian and Ukrainian-speaking community in Valencia. A distinct, defensible niche — not a limitation.

**Studio partner:** Koritsia (ceramist-in-residence) — handles teaching, kiln, and social media. Central to prestige-building.

---

## 1. Studio — Prestige Building

### Skill Ladder (3 tiers)

| Tier | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Open to all | Standard classes, welcoming front door |
| Intermediate | Natural progression | Wheel, glaze chemistry, technical classes |
| Advanced / Resident | Application + Koritsia endorsement | Own key, dedicated kiln slots, mentorship, early retreat access |

Gating the top tier pulls students upward without restricting entry. Names TBD — avoid "Level 1/2/3."

### Guest Artist Residencies
- 1–2 guest ceramicists per year
- Documented well (photos, process, story) — not hard-sold
- Gives advanced members new technique and engagement
- Over time: "Saged" becomes associated with a lineage of serious ceramicists

### Social Proof Mechanics
- **Quarterly open studio nights** — members display work, peers see serious practice
- **Brand drops** — limited studio-made pieces, advanced members get first access
- **Two-track Instagram** — beginner-friendly content (top of funnel) + advanced technique / guest artist thread (prestige signal)

### Growth (non-ad)
- Referrals from advanced members who bring culturally aligned people
- Personal DM invites for new offerings — not broadcast announcements
- Existing regulars are the warm list for everything: retreats, drops, events

## 2. Finca Retreat Venue — Build Plan

**Location:** Carcaixent finca, ~4.5 hectares, citrus grove (Clemenules + Navel), existing house under renovation. Land: suelo rústico, subject to LOTUP.

### Uses
1. Ceramic retreats (primary — Saged-run)
2. Coding/work gatherings
3. Annual fruit-picking / agrotourism days
4. Third-party venue rental (yoga, wellness, tantra groups)

### Building Programme

| Space | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Large table room (clay/handwork) | ~50 m² | 10–12 people |
| Pottery wheel room | ~38 m² | 6 wheels |
| Kiln room + ventilation | ~17 m² | — |
| Bathroom + prep kitchen | ~15 m² | — |
| Accommodation (6 ensuite rooms) | ~145 m² | Sleeps 12 |
| Event/practice room | ~80 m² | Movement, yoga, larger events |
| Natural pool (biopiscina) | ~35 m² footprint | Plant filtration, no chlorine |
| **Total (excl. pool)** | **~345 m²** | — |

### Cost Estimate

| Scenario | Rate | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Low | €1,500/m² | ~€510,000 |
| Mid | €2,250/m² | ~€765,000 |
| High | €3,000/m² | ~€1,020,000 |

Pool: +€15,000–22,000. **Target: €500,000.**

### Phased Build

**Stage 1 — Revenue core** (build first)
- Workshop rooms + 6 ensuite accommodation: ~265 m², ~€400,000
- Unlocks: venue rental + Saged retreats
- Conservative Year 1 net: ~€36,000/year

**Stage 2 — Event/practice room**
- ~80 m², ~€120,000–240,000
- Fund from Stage 1 operating profit

**Stage 3 — Natural pool**
- ~35 m², €15,000–22,000
- Add last, purely experiential

### Revenue Projections (conservative, Year 1)

| Stream | Gross | Net |
|---|---|---|
| Venue rental (4 bookings/yr, 5-day, €300/day) | €24,000 | ~€18,000 |
| Saged ceramic retreats (2/yr, 12 people, 5-day, €300/day) | €36,000 | ~€18,000 |
| Grove tourism days (~8 days/yr, 15 people, €30) | €3,600 | ~€2,800 |
| Honey course (1x/yr) | €1,680 | ~€1,100 |
| Nutrition course (2x/yr) | €3,600 | ~€1,800 |

Note: €41,700 net does not fully cover debt service on €500k at market rates. Finca is supported by household income (Alisher + grove + studio) during ramp-up.

### Financing Options (in order)
1. Hipoteca rústica — rural mortgage, LTV ~50–60%, rate ~3.5–4.5%
2. ICO-backed loan — ask Sabadell about rural/agrotourism ICO lines
3. LEADER/GAL grants — partial funding, confirm Carcaixent eligibility first
4. Family capital (parents, Latvia) — loan vs gift vs formal investment vehicle TBD

## 3. Studio → Finca Funnel

- Studio regulars are the primary warm list for retreat bookings — invite personally
- Host informal "first look" open house at finca before construction starts (wine + clay, low-key)
- Document the finca build on Instagram over 9–18 months — makes early followers feel like insiders
- Frame retreats as "come stay with us" — not a separate product, the same community deeper
- Koritsia as natural co-host (students already trust her)
- First retreat cohort: "bring a friend" mechanic, invite-only
