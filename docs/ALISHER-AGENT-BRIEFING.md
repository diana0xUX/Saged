## 5. Meta ads — full history

### Phase 0 — before May 11, 2026 (pre-audit)

- **€143.43 total** spent on 9 boosted Instagram posts  
- Result: **0 workshop bookings**  
- Root causes (from AUDIT.md): wrong objectives (MESSAGES/LINK_CLICKS, never Sales), no Pixel, no landing page, no language targeting, geo scattered (some all-Spain, some all-EU), no Custom Audiences  
- CTR was 1.97% and CPC was €0.17 — creative actually caught attention, funnel just dead-ended in an IG profile with no booking action

### Phase 1 — May 12 → Jun 9, 2026 (locale bug era)

- Rebuilt campaign with Sales objective, RU + UA separate ad sets, bilingual landing page live  
- **BUT**: locale IDs were wrong. RU ad set targeted locale 10 = Italian (not Russian). UA ad set targeted locale 37 = Bulgarian (not Ukrainian).  
- €141.89 spent showing Russian/Ukrainian creative to Italians and Bulgarians in Valencia  
- **0 bookings from this spend**  
- Fixed Jun 9: runtime `verify_locale()` guard added to scripts

### Phase 2 — Jun 9 → Jul 20, 2026 (Egypt geo bug era)

- Locale fixed. Campaigns rebuilt.  
- **BUT**: city key for Valencia was wrong. All 7 ad sets used key `668776` = Shubra Tana, Egypt. Every euro reached people in Egypt.  
- €95.46 spent targeting Egypt  
- **0 possible bookings from this spend**  
- Fixed Jul 20: city key verification added, correct Valencia key = `699854`

### Phase 3 — Jul 20, 2026 → now (first real delivery)

- All campaigns now correctly configured: OUTCOME_TRAFFIC objective, Valencia Spain geo (699854), correct locales (RU=17, UA=52)  
- As of Jul 31 (today): 2 incorrect OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT campaigns paused (kids €10/day + Sunday Slowdown €2/day). All remaining active = OUTCOME_TRAFFIC.

### What Meta data can and can't tell us

Meta campaign dashboard shows **0 conversions every month**. This does NOT mean ads don't work. Three reasons for broken attribution:
1. Custom conversion (Schedule → Purchase, ID `980444324695740`) has not been verified active in Meta Events Manager — flagged every weekly report, never confirmed fixed.
2. EU consent banner: visitors who decline cookies don't fire the Pixel.
3. The audience books via DM (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram), not via the Stripe link — so no `Purchase` event fires even for converted customers.

**Evidence ads DO correlate with revenue**:
- Feb (no ads): €80 income
- Jun (first month of correct RU+UA locale targeting): €1,123 income — best month
- Pattern observed: ads running → guests come in via DM

## 10. What has and hasn't worked

### What worked
- **Telegram DM with Cal.com link** → first booking, highest conversion rate observed
- **June 2026 (first correct locale targeting)** → best income month (€1,123)
- **DM-first conversion path** consistently outperforms click-to-checkout for this audience and ticket size (€60 community offer, warm diaspora community)
- **Coworking recurring revenue** — Ekaterina's €200/month provides baseline
- **B2B from word-of-mouth** — Lavin Media found them organically (€300 May)
- **Drop-in class (€30)** — lower commitment entry point, observed converting to repeat customers

### What didn't work
- **Meta automated objectives (MESSAGES/LINK_CLICKS)** — 0 bookings from €143 pre-May spend
- **Boosted Instagram posts** — no funnel, no conversion event, ends in DM with no booking flow
- **Pixel + custom conversion** — never confirmed working; attribution is blind
- **Sales-objective campaigns** — ran correctly for only ~11 days (Jul 20–31), too early to evaluate
- **OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT campaigns** — paused July 31 (wrong objective, pays for likes)
- **Spanish and English ad sets** — very small budget (€0.90/day each), no signal yet

### What's unknown
- Whether correct geo + locale + TRAFFIC objective actually drives bookings (only 11 days of real data Jul 20–31)
- True conversion rate from Meta ad → DM → booked
- Which sessions have waitlists vs. empty seats
- Real demand for kids (camp format vs. drop-in vs. multi-session course)
- Vita's session booking rate (new, Jun 2026)

## 11. The biggest structural gap

**The studio earns money via DM, but tracks nothing.** There is no CRM, no lead log, no conversion tracking between "person DMed" and "person paid."

Right now Diana sees:
- Bank deposits (Stripe, Bizum)
- WhatsApp/Telegram conversations (manual)
- Meta Ads Manager (0 conversions — broken attribution)

She doesn't see:
- How many DMs turned into bookings (funnel conversion rate)
- Which channel sent which customer
- Which session times fill fastest
- Which product has the best margin contribution

Without this data, every decision about where to spend budget and effort is a guess.

## 13. What "a working profitable system" needs to look like

Based on all data above, the gaps between current state and profitability are:

**Revenue gap**: Need €1,935/month to break even. Best month was €1,123. Gap = ~€812/month minimum.

**Fastest path to closing that gap**:
1. Fill 4 more coworking spots: +€800/month (reaches break-even)  
   — OR —
2. David De Limón partnership: +€865/month (reaches break-even from merch alone)  
   — OR a combination —
3. More sessions per week (add Saturday for adults): potentially +€300–€600/month at current fill rates
4. Kids class on regular weekly schedule: unknown upside, likely €100–€300/month at €25/child

**Marketing efficiency**: The funnel (correct TRAFFIC campaigns → DM → conversion) has only been running correctly since July 20. True ROAS is unknown. May 2026 before the bug era had estimated 5.24× ROAS based on Diana's attribution — but this was a good month with B2B + organic income also in it.

**Key recommendation from existing data**: The RU/UA diaspora audience in Valencia does book via DM. The limiting factor is not demand — it's (a) awareness funnel quality, (b) session capacity, and (c) non-workshop revenue streams that provide recurring baseline. Fixing the coworking fill rate and starting the De Limón partnership are the two moves with the highest leverage and lowest marginal cost.
