# Coworking Rules — Saged.club Ceramic Studio

Canonical pricing + access rules for the studio coworking offering. **Authoritative source** — when the website, IG post, or DMs disagree with this file, this file wins.

Last updated: 2026-05-14

## Offer in one line
Monthly access to the ceramic studio for experienced potters. 5 spots. €200/month.

## Hours
- Daily 10:00 – 22:00
- In free time from masterclasses
- Usually busy: **Thursday and Saturday**
- Schedule shared with members via Google Calendar

## What's included in the €200/month
- A spot in the studio (workspace)
- One block (~10 kg) of white clay
- Clear glaze (transparent)
- Engobes and other glazes — **small quantities only**. For production runs, members buy their own materials.
- Access to tools and equipment

## Charged separately
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Firing | €6 per shelf |
| Extra clay | €1 per kg |
| Engobes / other glazes for production volumes | Member buys their own |

## Deposit
- €100 deposit on signup
- Refundable when the member leaves

## Member-led masterclasses (optional perk)
Members can run their own masterclasses in the studio. Terms:
- Member reimburses the studio for material costs (clay + glaze used)
- Revenue split: **50/50 with the studio**

## Audience
- **Primary**: experienced ceramicists / potters who need studio access without owning a kiln
- **Secondary**: artists, designers needing a project space
- **Secondary**: teachers wanting a venue for their own classes
- **NOT directly**: beginners — they need the intro course first (see below)

## Intro course (entry point for newcomers)

A 4-class course that teaches a complete beginner how to use the studio independently. The graduation funnel into coworking.

- **Price**: €120 for the full 4-class course
- **Format**: 4 classes with a ceramist, small group
- **What's included**: all materials and firings included in the €120 — no extra fees during the course
- **Outcome**: graduate can either continue into coworking (€200/mo) or simply take their work and leave
- **Distinction from Thursday Koritsya workshop**: that's a 2-session experience (€60); the intro course is 4 sessions specifically designed to teach independent studio use
