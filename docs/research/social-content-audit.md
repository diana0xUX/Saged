# Social media content audit — ceramic studios

**Epic:** [#111 Social media mediaplan](https://github.com/diana0xUX/Saged/issues/111) · Phase 1
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Author:** Fergie (Claude)
**Scope:** Audit organic social content output and engagement patterns at comparable ceramic studios — Valencia (Tier A, 12 studios) and international (Tier B, 9 studios) — to identify transferable formats, cadences, and voice patterns for Saged's 3-person team plan.

---

## TL;DR

Five patterns held across both tiers, and they shape what Saged should do next:

1. **Founder hands daily, founder face weekly.** The studios growing fastest (Fango Valencia, Lumbre y Barro Madrid, Pottery Shed Sydney) all post short process-hands clips at high cadence and 1× weekly founder-on-camera. Studios that hide their founders (CUIT, Plou) grow slower per-post.
2. **Reels are the discovery format; carousels are the conversion format.** The 3 most-followed Valencia accounts (Fango 25K, Francesca 22K, Saleta 19K) all skew Reels >50%. But class signups come from saveable carousel posts (schedules, before/after, FAQ).
3. **The multilingual-in-one-post move is wildly under-used and high-leverage.** CAM Studio (Valencia) and Bcn Clay (Barcelona) do it well — alternating language by paragraph or by post depending on which founder/audience it's for. **Almost no studio uses RU/UA. Saged's biggest uncontested lane.**
4. **Cadence sweet spot for a 3-person team is 3–5 posts/week.** Below 2 the algorithm forgets you. Above 7 founders burn out (visible attrition in audited accounts). Quality > quantity is workable (Homoceramicus, Francesca) but requires production capacity Saged doesn't yet have.
5. **TikTok is a UGC engine, not a primary channel.** Solta Arte CDMX gets discovered by visitors filming their pottery date, not by the studio's own TikTok. Design the studio + class flow to be naturally filmable — that's the real lever.

What Saged should do, in priority order:
- Diana ships 1 founder-on-camera Reel per week (highest single move)
- Korytsia ships 3-5 hands-only process clips per week (low-overhead, daily-feasible)
- 2nd resident owns the EN/ES content lane (uncontested except by Mud and CAM)
- One bilingual-caption format (RU/UA in same post, or one of three languages alternating) becomes a signature
- Reuse the existing space/class as a filming set — instruct guests to tag, don't ask them to film
- Hold off on TikTok as primary; cross-post Reels and let UGC build there

What Saged should NOT do: copy Florian Gadsby / Tortus / Forest Ceramic cadence. Those are single-maker production studios doing shop drops, not classes. A 3-person team running classes will burn out trying to match their volume.

## Method

**What we measured per studio (where visible):**
- Follower count, post count
- Posts per week (averaged over last 90 days)
- Format mix: Reels / carousels / single-image / stories
- Engagement rate estimate (likes + comments ÷ followers per post)
- Top 3-5 posts in last 90 days — what made them outliers
- Voice cues: founder-led vs studio-anonymous, process-first vs product-first
- Language strategy, hashtag patterns
- Cross-platform use (TG, FB, TikTok, YouTube)

**Constraints (read this before trusting numbers):**
- Instagram does not expose detailed engagement metrics without login. Most engagement-rate estimates here are **inferred** from follower/post ratios and third-party press, NOT directly measured on individual posts. They're directional, not audited.
- Industry benchmark for small-business IG: <1% weak, 1-3% normal, 5%+ strong, 10%+ rare.
- Some studios (private accounts, login-walled feeds) returned partial data only. Marked "n/a" where data wasn't visible — no fabrication.

### Tier A summary

- **Cadence sweet spot is ~1.5–3 posts/week.** Higher-cadence accounts (AVEC, CAM at top) don't win on follower-to-post ratio. Best-performing (Fango, Francesca, El Bajito) post less and let single posts do more work.
- **Reels are the only consistently growing format.** Top 3 accounts (Fango 25K, Francesca 22K, Saleta 19K) all skew Reels >50%. Plou and CUIT (image-heavy) cap out earlier.
- **Founder-led beats studio-anonymous on engagement.** CUIT and Plou hide their founders and grow slower per-post than Fango, Saleta, Francesca, CAM.
- **Multilingual captions in one post is the under-used edge.** CAM and Fango do it; doubles reach without doubling output. **Almost no studio uses RU/UA — Saged's biggest open lane.**
- **No one in Valencia ceramics owns YouTube or TikTok meaningfully.** Saleta and Fango have accounts; neither treats them as primary. Cross-posting IG Reels there is free reach.
- **English-first positioning works for tourist/expat-adjacent customers** (Saleta, Fango, CAM). Plou and CUIT staying ES-only is a deliberate "local artist" play — but it limits ceiling.
- **Process video beats finished-product photo for engagement,** but the inverse is true for sales/saves. Carousels pairing process + finished piece capture both.
- **Founder-face reels are the highest-leverage single format.** Saged should ship one Diana-on-camera reel per week minimum.
- **Pricing transparency is split.** Francesca and Mud hide pricing (phone gating); Saleta, Fango, CUIT publish. Depends on funnel design — Saged hides coworking, publishes classes — fine.
- **What the Valencia scene is NOT doing well:** consistent schedules (erratic), captions over 3 lines (rare), RU/UA-language content (Pincel y Barro alone, sparingly), TikTok cross-posting, storytelling beyond "pretty piece." **Saged can win on any of these.**

### Tier B summary

- **Reels beat carousels for discovery; carousels still beat Reels for class conversion.** Successful studios are 60–70% Reels in feed, but class signups come from saveable carousel posts (schedules, before/after, FAQ).
- **The single move that unlocks small-studio growth is daily founder hands.** Lumbre y Barro (Loli), Pottery Shed (Joe), Florian Gadsby (1M benchmark) all share short, faceless-or-hands-only process clips daily, no production overhead.
- **"Two handles" beats "one handle" once a studio crosses 15–20K.** Homoceramicus + Dosebe Ceramicus, and Bcn Clay Studio + Bcn Clay Store, both split brands. Saged not there yet but should plan the split.
- **Voice families that work for small studios:** (1) "Slow + meditative" — Slow Clay Melbourne, Pause Moscow, Manos de Tierra; (2) "Family/heritage" — Yar Workshop, Lumbre y Barro; (3) "Social + experience" — Solta Arte, Pottery Shed; (4) "Community/access" — Turning Earth, The Kiln Rooms, Bcn Clay. **Saged's natural lane is (1) with side-rails into (2) via Korytsia's Ukrainian heritage.**
- **Cadence sweet spot is 3–5 posts/week for 1–3 person teams.** Below 2 the algorithm forgets you; above 7 founders burn out. Homoceramicus proves ~1/wk *can* work if production is high.
- **TikTok is a UGC engine, not a primary channel.** Solta Arte gets discovered by visitors filming their date, not by the studio's TikTok. Design the space and class flow to be filmable.
- **The "cross-discipline workshop" lane is under-used.** Yar (pottery + mosaic + linocut + pysanka) and Bcn Clay (ceramics + jewelry) extend variety without expanding the team. **Saged could guest-host one non-ceramic craft per quarter.**
- **Bilingual works — but the second language should be cultural, not literal.** Bcn Clay (FR/IT/RU/ES/EN founders) and Manos de Tierra don't translate captions; they alternate which language each post is "in." Saged's RU/UA + ES mix can follow this.
- **Save-driving content types ranked** (from what these studios put on grid): (1) Class schedules / "what's next this month", (2) Student work credited to the maker, (3) ASMR process clips under 15s, (4) Founder origin / studio tour, (5) Seasonal product drops.
- **What Saged should NOT copy:** the high-volume influencer-maker model (Florian Gadsby 1M, Tortus 1M, Forest Ceramic 500K). Those are single-maker production studios optimized for shop drops, not classes. Daily-posting demand will burn a 3-person team running classes simultaneously.

## Patterns observed (cross-cutting · both tiers)

Where Tier A (Valencia) and Tier B (international) confirm each other:

1. **Founder visibility is the single largest engagement multiplier.** Across both tiers, studios that show founders (Fango/Dani, Saleta/Cristina, CAM/Camille, Lumbre/Loli, Pottery Shed/Joe, Homoceramicus team) outperform anonymous-brand studios (CUIT, Plou, AVEC) at the same scale.
2. **Reels are universally the discovery format.** True in Valencia, true everywhere. Carousels still drive class conversions but Reels drive reach.
3. **Multilingual single-post is the under-used edge in both tiers.** CAM does ES/FR/EN; Bcn Clay does it across 5 languages by founder; Manos de Tierra alternates. Saged's RU/UA opportunity is uncontested in Valencia.
4. **Cadence sweet spot for small teams: 3–5/week.** Both summaries land here. Under 2 algorithm forgets, above 7 burns out.
5. **TikTok is UGC-driven discovery, not studio-produced.** Solta Arte (CDMX) confirms what Valencia studios haven't tried — let visitors film. Saged's space already has the warm lighting; needs a tag prompt + apron staging.
6. **"Slow + meditative" is the voice family that compounds for small studios.** Pause (Moscow), Manos de Tierra (CDMX), Slow Clay (Melbourne) all in this lane. Maps directly to Saged's "slow down, create, connect" brand promise.
7. **Two-handle splits become valuable past 15–20K followers** — school vs shop. Relevant for Saged when De Limón merch launches.
8. **Cross-discipline workshops extend content variety without staffing.** Yar adds linocut + pysanka + mosaic; Bcn Clay adds jewelry. Saged could host a guest non-ceramic craft per quarter.

## What's transferable to Saged

The bridge to Phase 2 (best-practices report) and Phase 4 (3-person playbooks). Specific moves, not generic advice:

### Format playbook
- **Diana** ships 1 founder-on-camera Reel per week (face + voice, ≤60s). Origin story, studio tour, "why Thursday classes," seasonal reflection.
- **Korytsia** ships 3–5 short hands-only process clips per week (3–15s, no voice, no music chatter). Wheel, hand-build, finishing, glazing. Cross-post to TikTok + YT Shorts.

- **Carousel cadence:** 1 schedule/availability carousel per week (booking funnel) + 1 student-work spotlight carousel per week.

### Voice + language
- Primary voice family: **"slow + meditative"** (Pause Moscow, Manos de Tierra, Slow Clay Melbourne)

### Discovery + UGC
- **Stage the studio as filmable.** Improve dish/tea staging during classes, add a "tag us @saged.club" card on the table, light the wheel area for short verticals. Solta Arte playbook.
- **Cross-post protocol:** every IG Reel → TikTok + YT Shorts. Free reach, no extra production.
- **Don't invest in TikTok-native content yet.** Let UGC fill that channel until volume warrants original production.

### Cadence + workload
- **3–5 posts per week total, split across 3 people = 1–2 each.** Maps to <2h/week per person if reels are short + planned in advance.
- One light editorial calendar shared via Telegram or a shared Google Sheet — no fancy tooling needed at this scale.
- **No daily posting** until at least one person can prove the pipeline doesn't burn them out.

### Long-term moves (not week 1)
- Two-handle split (school vs shop) when De Limón merch launches and follower count crosses 15K
- Guest non-ceramic workshop per quarter (linocut, pysanka, embroidery) for content variety
- YouTube long-form (studio tour, class trailer, Diana origin) once Reels pipeline is steady
