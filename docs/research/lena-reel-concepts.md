## Part 1 — Pattern research at 6 reference accounts

> **Method note:** Instagram doesn't render reel/profile pages via fetch (only logos load), so per-reel view counts aren't directly visible. Patterns triangulated from Google-indexed reel captions, cross-posted TikTok metrics (Fango's @fango.ceramicaa is the only Spanish-cluster studio with public TikTok), Domestika/press profiles, podcast interviews (The Potters Cast ep. 1202 for Fango; The Mentor podcast for Joe Darling), and YouTube cross-posts (Lumbre y Barro). Where patterns are read off thumbnails/captions vs. watched directly, that's flagged in source notes at the end.

### Yar Workshop · @yar_workshop (Kyiv, 19K)

**Pattern 1: Founder/family voiceover over hands-at-wheel**
- **Hook (0-3s):** wide-ish workshop interior or hand pressing into spinning clay; small face crop in corner of founder speaking Ukrainian.
- **Length:** medium (15-30s) · **Audio:** Ukrainian voiceover, soft instrumental bed, NOT trending audio
- **Anatomy:** caption-style opener ("Знайомство зазвичай починають, коли майстерня тільки відкрилась…") → cut to hands → cut to founder face → cut to finished piece
- **On-screen text:** headline-only Ukrainian (1 line, top of frame), no full subtitles
- **Why it works:** UA-language confessional opener signals "family talking to you, not a brand" — high diaspora trust. Voice carries the slowness, wheel carries the visual.

**Pattern 2: "Come to your place of comfort" invitation reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** soft-light interior of the workshop, often piece mid-creation on a wheel
- **Length:** short to medium (10-20s) · **Audio:** ambient + soft music, no voiceover, no trending
- **Anatomy:** establishing shot → close-up of hands or tools → finished shelf → text-card invitation
- **On-screen text:** single-line invitation ("Твоє місце затишку і творчості" / "your place of comfort and creativity") + sign-up CTA
- **Why it works:** sells the *room*, not the technique. For Slavic-speaking audience far from home, "затишок" (cozy/comfort, untranslatable) is the dog-whistle word. Mood postcard.

**Pattern 3: Family/heritage multi-craft showcase**
- **Hook (0-3s):** quick cuts between pottery, mosaic, linocut workstations
- **Length:** medium (20-30s) · **Audio:** instrumental, UA folk-adjacent (inferred)
- **Anatomy:** wheel hands → mosaic tiles → linocut press → group shot of objects
- **Why it works:** positions studio as *traditions keeper*, not pottery shop. Breadth signals heritage stewardship.

### Lumbre y Barro · @lumbreybarro (Madrid, 25K)

**Pattern 1: Daily founder-hands wheel-throwing close-up**
- **Hook (0-3s):** Loli's hands already on the clay, often mid-pull. Camera ~30cm from wheel
- **Length:** medium (15-30s) · **Audio:** ambient wheel sound + soft music, no voiceover. Cross-posted to YouTube as longer tutorials
- **Anatomy:** centering → opening → pulling → trim. One full mini-process per reel
- **On-screen text:** minimal — sometimes Spanish caption naming the piece
- **Why it works:** "23 years at the wheel" credibility shows in *speed and quiet confidence* of her hands — viewers feel they're watching a master. Format Saged should clone for Korytsia.

**Pattern 2: Student outcome / piece-handoff reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** finished piece held in student's hands, or shelf of fresh bisque
- **Length:** short (8-15s) · **Audio:** music only
- **Anatomy:** finished reveal → student smiling → Loli in background of frame
- **On-screen text:** "Hecho a mano por…" + first name
- **Why it works:** social proof at human scale. Loli stays in background, protecting master/student hierarchy without coldness.

**Pattern 3: "Welcome to the studio" door-open reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** front door or shop window opening into warm interior
- **Length:** short (<10s) · **Audio:** soft music, sometimes a single trending Spanish indie sound
- **Anatomy:** door/threshold → pan across shelves → close on hand placing new piece
- **Why it works:** turns brick-and-mortar walk-in into a reel hook. For locals = converts; for diaspora = sells the fantasy of "a place that exists."

### Manos de Tierra · @manosdetierramx (CDMX + Monterrey, 14K)

**Pattern 1: Philosophy-card cold open + slow craft footage**
- **Hook (0-3s):** full-screen Spanish phrase on clay-textured background ("Como el cauce…" / like a riverbed; "Toma un momento…")
- **Length:** medium (15-25s) · **Audio:** music only, acoustic guitar or piano, no voiceover
- **Anatomy:** text card (2-3s) → cut to hands working clay → cut to finished object on neutral surface → fade
- **On-screen text:** the philosophy card IS the reel's spine. Text is the hook, not the visual
- **Why it works:** Spanish ceramic audience treats studio as a *philosophy account first, studio second*. Functions like a quote-card you'd save — high save/share rate, IG ranks. **Saged's slow/meditative voice template, fully realized.**

**Pattern 2: "Descubre cómo…" invitation reels (workshop CTA)**
- **Hook (0-3s):** finished piece on table, candle, or natural light streaming in
- **Length:** short to medium (10-20s) · **Audio:** ambient + soft instrumental
- **Anatomy:** finished beauty shot → quick process montage → end card with workshop dates
- **Why it works:** same slow visual language as Pattern 1 pivoted to conversion. No tonal whiplash between philosophy and selling.

**Pattern 3: "Así se siente…" / sensory-first reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** close-up of hands sinking into wet clay, or water being poured
- **Length:** short (8-15s) · **Audio:** ambient + minimal music, ASMR-adjacent
- **Why it works:** ASMR pottery format routed through brand "feeling" language, not the trend's "satisfying" language.

### Pause · @pause.msc (Moscow, 11K)

**Pattern 1: Minimal-text mood reels ("atmosphere PAUSE")**
- **Hook (0-3s):** ultra-clean studio shot — concrete floor, single wheel, daylight. Often nobody on screen for first 1-2s
- **Length:** short (8-15s) · **Audio:** ambient + minimal electronic/lo-fi music, no trending audio
- **Anatomy:** empty room → hands enter frame → wheel spins up → fade
- **On-screen text:** literally one word ("atmosphere PAUSE," "пауза"), sometimes none
- **Why it works:** scarcity of text + design-forward composition makes feed read like a Pinterest mood board, not a workshop ad. RU design-literate audience reads "minimal = premium." Design discipline lever Saged can borrow.

**Pattern 2: "Date format" candlelit class reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** two pairs of hands at adjacent wheels, candlelight, low ambient
- **Length:** medium (15-25s) · **Audio:** soft music, occasionally one trending RU indie track
- **Anatomy:** wide of couple at wheels → close-up of hands meeting in clay → finished pair side by side
- **On-screen text:** "Свидание в студии" / "date at the studio" + price/time
- **Why it works:** sells *experience-with-someone-you-love* over solo craft. Solves a Friday-night problem, not a hobby problem.

**Pattern 3: Object-as-still-life reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** single finished vessel on textured surface, daylight only
- **Length:** short (<10s) · **Audio:** silence-heavy ambient, almost no music
- **Anatomy:** static shot → slow camera push or single rotation → cut to logo card
- **Why it works:** trains algorithm to feed Pause to design-aesthetic accounts, not pottery-hobby accounts. Product-as-content with restraint that says "gallery" not "shop."

### Fango · @fango.valencia (Valencia, 25K)

**Pattern 1: Mini-torno / tiny pottery cross-posts** ⚠️ AVOID
- Mini wheel making thumb-sized pieces, size mismatch is the joke
- Trending Spanish/Latin audio, lots of emojis, hashtag-heavy
- *Why noted:* Fango's *trend-chasing* lane — Saged should **not** copy this but should understand competitively.

**Pattern 2: "Pottery gym" — workshop floor energy reels** ⚠️ AVOID
- Wide of multiple students at wheels, music in room, upbeat Spanish indie/Latin pop
- Reframes pottery as *reps not zen* — gym membership model
- *Why noted:* Brilliant for Fango's positioning; opposite of Saged's "slow + meditative."

**Pattern 3: Founder profile / "meet Daniele" reels** ✅ RELEVANT
- **Hook (0-3s):** Daniele's face + hands at wheel, black apron
- **Length:** medium (15-30s) · **Audio:** voiceover in EN or ES narrating his Italy → Valencia journey
- **Anatomy:** face shot → hands working → b-roll of finished pieces → cut back to face
- **On-screen text:** EN subtitles (international audience)
- **Why it works:** outsider-founder story (Italian in Valencia) is exactly the Diana-in-Valencia template. EN subtitles widen past locals. **Most relevant Fango pattern for Saged — same demographic arbitrage.**

### The Pottery Shed · @thepotteryshed_ (Sydney, 15K)

**Pattern 1: Process tutorial reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** Joe's hands on a piece, often at *trim* stage where form is recognizable
- **Length:** medium to long (20-40s) · **Audio:** Joe's voiceover (American accent), light music bed
- **Anatomy:** quick demo of one technique → close-up of hands → cut to finished result
- **Why it works:** Joe's *teacher-voice* (40K students taught) is the asset. American accent in Sydney = mild novelty + perceived authority. Tutorial = high save rate.

**Pattern 2: Studio invitation / "we have spots" reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** wide of warehouse-style studio, students at wheels
- **Length:** short (8-15s) · **Audio:** Joe direct-to-camera or upbeat music
- **Why it works:** unfussy, transactional, Joe's warmth softens the ask. Sydney audience is conversion-ready; *not* a great fit for Saged's diaspora audience who often can't book.

**Pattern 3: Finished-piece set / "drop" reels**
- **Hook (0-3s):** fresh batch of bisque or glazed work lined up
- **Length:** short (<10s) · **Audio:** music only
- **Why it works:** social proof for class quality + shop merchandising in one reel.

## Cross-account pattern summary

**Formats appearing across multiple studios — highest-confidence bets for Saged:**

- **Hands-only at wheel, music bed, 15-30s.** Universal floor (Lumbre y Barro, Manos de Tierra, Pause, Pottery Shed, Fango). Korytsia hands as the asset.
- **Philosophy/quote-card cold open into slow craft footage.** Manos de Tierra owns this; Pause has a visual version; Yar implies it. **Highest-leverage Saged-specific format** — bilingual RU/UA quote cards over hand footage. Saves and shares are the loop.
- **Founder-face voiceover narrating a moment.** Yar (UA voiceover) + Pottery Shed (accented EN) + Fango (Italian outsider). Diana on camera speaking RU over her hands = diaspora-trust unlock.
- **"Welcome / come to your place of comfort" invitation reels.** Yar's "затишок," Lumbre y Barro's door-open, Pause's empty-room-then-hands. Sells the *room as refuge*. Direct map to Saged's slow voice.
- **Student outcome / piece-handoff reels.** Lumbre y Barro, Fango, Pottery Shed all run this. Conversion driver.

**Formats unique to one studio — possible Saged distinctive lane:**

- **Manos de Tierra's philosophy-card-first reels.** Nobody else leads with text-on-clay-texture as the visual hook. Underused in RU/UA ceramic space — Saged could own it bilingually.
- **Pause's near-silent still-life reels.** Discipline of "almost no text, almost no music, single object" is unusual + design-forward. Trains algorithm toward design audience.
- **Yar's three-craft showcase.** Doesn't transfer if Saged is pottery-only, but the *family-stewardship framing* (this is what we've been doing, here it all is) does — adaptable as "this is one day at our workshop."

**What NOT to copy:**

- ❌ Fango's mini-torno / size-shock novelty + trending-audio cross-posts. Highest views in the set, but tonally violent against Saged's voice. Burns brand for one-time reach.
- ❌ Fango's "pottery gym" reps-not-zen framing. Opposite of Saged's positioning.
- ❌ Pottery Shed's transactional "we have spots" CTA reels — fine occasionally, but their audience is conversion-ready locals; Saged's is partly diaspora who can't book. Lead with philosophy/hands; sell sparingly.
- ❌ Loud trending audio in general. Five of six studios avoid it (only Fango leans in). Slow ceramic audience is trained on ambient/instrumental beds.

### Slot 3 (proposed): Korytsia hands + UA voiceover heritage

- **Pattern source:** Yar Pattern 1 (founder voiceover over hands) + Lumbre y Barro daily-hands
- **Hook (0-3s):** Korytsia's hands already on wet clay, mid-pull, soft side light
- **Length:** 20s
- **Audio:** Korytsia voiceover in UA (1 sentence — a memory, her grandmother, learning to throw, or what clay means to her) over wheel ambient
- **Anatomy:** hands mid-pull (4s) → cut to wider of Korytsia at wheel without showing face (6s) → cut to UA text card (3s) → cut to finished piece on shelf → fade
- **Language strategy:** UA voiceover · RU caption translation as primary post text · EN caption as secondary
- **Caption template:**
  > UA: Бабуся вчила мене руками, не словами. Глина пам'ятає.
  > RU: Бабушка учила меня руками, не словами. Глина помнит.
  > EN: My grandmother taught me with her hands, not her words. Clay remembers.
  >
  > Кориця · керамика Saged · @saged.club
- **Post-purpose:** saves + community resonance. Establishes Korytsia as the heritage anchor.
- **Why fits audience:** matches Yar Workshop's diaspora-trust formula; closest thing to a "Ukrainian woman in Valencia" voice in this category. Format Korytsia can replicate cheaply weekly afterward.

### Slot 4 (proposed): Bilingual philosophy card → hands

- **Pattern source:** Manos de Tierra Pattern 1 (philosophy-card cold open)
- **Hook (0-3s):** full-screen RU+UA phrase on clay-textured background, no other visual
- **Length:** 18s
- **Audio:** music-only (acoustic guitar or piano, slow)
- **Anatomy:** text card RU + UA (3s) → cut to hands working clay (8s) → cut to finished object on neutral wood surface (5s) → fade with subtle Saged logo
- **Language strategy:** RU + UA in same reel via the text card itself; EN translation only in caption
- **Caption template (the text-card phrase + same phrase in caption):**
  > RU: Замедлись. Глина не торопится.
  > UA: Сповільнись. Глина не поспішає.
  >
  > EN: Slow down. Clay isn't in a hurry.
  >
  > @saged.club · C/ de les Cuines, 8 · Valencia
- **Post-purpose:** saves + shares. Quote-card structure is what people save for later, screenshot, send.
- **Why fits audience:** the bilingual quote-card format is **uncontested in Valencia** (audit confirmed: nobody does RU+UA in same reel). Saged-distinctive lane. Daily-content template Korytsia or Diana can replicate weekly.

### Slot 5 (proposed): Studio door open / "your place of slowness"

- **Pattern source:** Lumbre y Barro Pattern 3 (door-open) + Yar Pattern 2 ("затишок" framing)
- **Hook (0-3s):** Saged's front door opening from the inside POV, light spilling in from the street, hands of person entering visible
- **Length:** 12-15s
- **Audio:** soft music (instrumental, warm), ambient street → studio audio transition
- **Anatomy:** door opens (3s) → single pan through studio interior, shelves of work in frame (5s) → close on a hand placing a finished piece on shelf (3s) → text card → fade
- **Language strategy:** RU + UA text card · EN caption translation only
- **Caption template:**
  > RU: Тихое место в старой Валенсии. Без спешки.
  > UA: Тихе місце в старій Валенсії. Без поспіху.
  > EN: A quiet place in old town Valencia. No rush.
  >
  > C/ de les Cuines, 8 · @saged.club
- **Post-purpose:** mood / awareness. Sells the room as refuge. Reuses well for ads.
- **Why fits audience:** maps directly to Saged's brand promise ("a place to slow down, create, connect"). The "затишок" word in the UA-spec version is the dog-whistle for the diaspora audience. Works as ad creative too.

### Slot 6 (proposed): From hands to fire (process montage)

- **Pattern source:** Lumbre y Barro daily-hands extended into one full mini-process
- **Hook (0-3s):** wet clay between fingers, extreme close-up, almost abstract
- **Length:** 25s
- **Audio:** ambient wheel sound + very minimal music bed (Pause-style restraint)
- **Anatomy:** wet clay close (3s) → wheel pulling form (5s) → trimming bowl (3s) → kiln door opening with bisque ware visible (3s) → glazing brushstroke (4s) → finished glazed piece on wood (5s) → fade. No text overlay.
- **Language strategy:** silent reel — all-language reach. Caption RU + UA + EN.
- **Caption template:**
  > RU: От руки до огня — две недели, потому что глина не торопится.
  > UA: Від руки до вогню — два тижні, тому що глина не поспішає.
  > EN: From hands to fire — two weeks, because clay isn't in a hurry.
  >
  > Saged · керамика по четвергам
- **Post-purpose:** saves + universal-audience reach. ASMR-adjacent, exports across all language groups, viable as ad creative.
- **Why fits audience:** silent + tactile is the universal pottery language. Bypasses RU/UA/EN constraint — anyone can watch. Educational micro-story about the actual ceramic process (which is the truth of why classes take 2 weeks).

### Slot 7 (proposed): Object as still-life

- **Caption template:**

- **Why fits audience:** Saged's RU + ES design-literate audience reads "minimal = considered" not "minimal = empty." Builds the kind of audience that eventually buys De Limón merch.

### Slot 8 (proposed): Student handoff (the human moment)

- **Pattern source:** Lumbre y Barro Pattern 2 (student outcome)
- **Hook (0-3s):** finished bowl held in cupped hands of a student (no face yet)
- **Length:** 15s
- **Audio:** music only (warm acoustic)

- **Language strategy:** RU caption primary, UA secondary, EN tertiary
- **Caption template:**
  > RU: Сделала своими руками. Через две недели — заберёт домой.
  > UA: Зробила своїми руками. За два тижні — забере додому.
  > EN: Made by her own hands. In two weeks — she takes it home.
  >
  > @saged.club · керамика по четвергам
- **Post-purpose:** social proof + DM-driver. Aspirational without being salesy.
- **Why fits audience:** familiar diaspora pattern of "look what's possible." Korytsia in the background of frame protects the master/student hierarchy without being cold (Lumbre y Barro move).
- **⚠️ Requires consent:** find a student who's OK with appearing on camera. Easier on a Thursday class day; brief Korytsia to ask in advance.
