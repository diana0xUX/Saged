Description

A ceramics class for beginners and those with some experience. We combine wheel throwing and hand building techniques in a small group — a maximum of 5 people per session.


Each session ends with your own piece, which goes into the kiln after two sessions. Koritsa guides from the first touch to the final glaze — at your own pace, without rushing.


No experience needed. All materials are included.
