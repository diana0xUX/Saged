About

Diana is the founder of Saged — a ceramics studio and cultural space in the old city of Valencia. She started working with clay during the pandemic and hasn't stopped since. Saged grew from her own need for a place to slow down, make things with hands, and find community in a new city.


At the studio she runs ritual workshops: talisman making, seasonal ceremonies, and creative gatherings where clay meets intention. Her practice sits at the intersection of craft, design, and the kind of meaning-making you can only do with your hands.
