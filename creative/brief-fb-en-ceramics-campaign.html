Why This Campaign Exists

English-speaking residents in Valencia have no organic way to discover Saged. The Russian and Ukrainian audience reaches the studio through Koritsia's personal network. English-speakers don't. A paid ad is their only path.

This is not a brand awareness campaign. The only metric that matters is someone opening WhatsApp and writing: "Hi, when is the next ceramics class?"

The best-performing ad on record for this studio — an English-language video, "Saged.club is for..." — achieved CTR 5.17% and CPC €0.01 for three consecutive weeks. That outperformed Russian/Ukrainian variants by 5–6×. This campaign continues that bet with static image formats.

The Audience

English-speaking expats and long-term residents of Valencia, 25–45. Not tourists.

They're looking for something to do that isn't another bar or gym. They haven't found "their thing" in Valencia yet. Ceramics sounds interesting but they've never tried it. They're comfortable spending €60 on a real experience. When they see an ad, they DM — they don't click through to a booking page. They want to ask a question first.

What they feel before seeing the ad: mild FOMO about not having found their people yet.
What the ad should make them feel: this is the obvious answer.

Visual Direction

The reference: creative/library/05-ads/koritsia-ceramics/1080x1350-preview.jpg — this is the Russian version that proved the design language works. Match its energy exactly.

The feeling: A door left slightly open. Warm light inside. Sounds of work. No pressure to enter. The piece you take home is lopsided and you love it more for that.

What this looks like on canvas

  • Warm cream background (#F9F4ED) — never white, never grey
  • Big confident headline in Costa font — two words max per line at display size
  • A real photo of someone working with clay — not a product shot, not stock
  • Orange price blob, visible without scrolling (#FF7A23)
  • WhatsApp number as the CTA — not "Book Now", not a website link
  • 3–5 illustrated brand figures scattered at the edges at opacity 0.45–0.55
  • Organic blob shapes, not geometric grids

What this is NOT

  • Spa / wellness aesthetic
  • Minimalist-cold white space with tiny sans-serif type
  • Stock photography energy — models pretending to make pottery
  • Banner-ad layout (text block dropped over a blurred photo)

Layout Rules

  • 3 focal points max. Headline + photo + price blob. Everything else supports these three.
  • Z-pattern. Top-left "SAGED CLUB" → logo top-right → diagonal across photo → price blob bottom-right. Design to this eye path.
  • Squint test. Half-close your eyes. The headline or the instructor's face must read first. If the address block or figures read first, the hierarchy is wrong.
  • Price always visible without scrolling. Bottom half, orange blob. If the viewer can't see €60 without scrolling, the layout fails.
  • Figures at edges only. 3–5 illustrated characters from creative/library/02-figures/ — corners and edges, opacity 0.45–0.55, never centred.
