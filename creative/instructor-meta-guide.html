Before you start

What this guide covers

Saged has run Meta (Instagram + Facebook) campaigns since March 2026. We've learned — sometimes expensively — what settings work for promoting ceramics workshops to audiences in Valencia. This guide tells you exactly how to set up a campaign for your classes, using the settings we've verified work, and warning you about the mistakes we made so you don't repeat them.

This is not a general Meta advertising tutorial. It is specific to: promoting classes in Valencia, to Russian-speaking, Ukrainian-speaking, and English-speaking residents, on a small budget (€50–€150/month).

Before running any ads: talk to Diana first. She manages the studio's Meta Business account and ad account. Your campaign will run under the studio account — this keeps billing, pixels, and brand consistency in one place.
The three rules that matter most
⚠️ Rule 1 — Never trust locale IDs from memory
In May 2026, a campaign launched with locale 10 labeled "Russian" and locale 37 labeled "Ukrainian." Both were wrong — locale 10 is Italian, locale 37 is Bulgarian. The campaign ran for 30 days showing Russian-language ceramic ads to Italians and Bulgarians in Valencia. €141.89 wasted, 0 bookings. Always verify locale IDs via the Meta API before use.
⚠️ Rule 2 — Never trust city keys from memory
In July 2026, all 7 active ad sets used city key 668776, which is Shubra Tana, Egypt — not Valencia. The correct Valencia city key is 699854. €95.46 spent reaching people in Egypt. Always verify geo keys via the Meta API, never from a document or memory.
✓ Rule 3 — Always create campaigns as PAUSED
Never launch a campaign live immediately. Create everything in PAUSED state, review all settings, then Diana activates. This prevents accidental spend before you've checked the geo, locale, and creative.
Step-by-step campaign setup
1
Campaign level settings
The top-level choices that define what Meta optimises for
Objective
OUTCOME_TRAFFIC Sends people to your landing page or booking link. DO NOT use OUTCOME_ENGAGEMENT — that pays for likes, comments and shares, not actual bookings or DMs. We learned this the hard way.
Campaign name
Saged · [Your Class Name] · [Language] · [Month Year] Example: "Saged · Ceramics with Koritsia · RU · Oct 2026". Consistent naming makes reporting readable and prevents confusion with other active campaigns.
Special ad categories
None Workshop classes are not housing, employment, or credit. Leave this blank.
Campaign budget
Set at ad set level, not campaign level. Leave CBO (Campaign Budget Optimisation) off. Gives you control over how much each language/audience spends. With CBO, Meta decides — and it tends to pour budget into the easy audience, not the right one.
Status
PAUSED — always. Activate manually after full review.
2
Ad set level — one per language
Create separate ad sets for Russian, Ukrainian, and English. Never merge them.
Never merge RU and UA into one ad set
Russian and Ukrainian audiences are different communities with different sensitivities. Merging them means your copy must try to speak to both — and usually speaks to neither. One ad set per language, always.
Ad set name
[Class Name] · RU · Valencia / [Class Name] · UA · Valencia / [Class Name] · EN · Valencia
Conversion location
Website (if sending to landing page) or Messaging apps (if CTA is WhatsApp DM) WhatsApp DM campaigns outperform landing page campaigns for this audience and price point. First confirmed Saged booking came via Telegram DM, not a landing page click.
Performance goal
Maximise number of link clicks (for traffic) or Maximise conversations (for WhatsApp)
Budget
€3–€5 / day per ad set — or a lifetime budget of €50–€70 for a 2–3 week push At €5/day you can reach 1,000–3,000 people in Valencia per day with a good creative. Don't over-spend early — run 1 week first, then check CTR before scaling.
Schedule
Run 2–3 weeks before class date. Stop 2 days before the class. Last-minute ads (under 48h before) rarely convert — people need time to consider and DM. Start early enough that there's a booking conversation window.
Bid strategy
Lowest cost without cap Lets Meta find cheapest clicks/conversations within your budget. Don't set a cost cap until you have 3+ weeks of data.
3
Audience targeting — the most critical part
Get this wrong and every euro goes to the wrong people. We spent €237 learning this.
Location
City: Valencia, Spain
City key: 699854 — verify via Meta API before every campaign
Radius: 17 km
Location type: People who live here only Do NOT use "recently in this location" — that catches tourists who will never book a workshop. We want residents. The correct Valencia key is 699854 — NOT 668776 which is a town in Egypt (we spent €95 on Egypt before finding this).
Language / locale
Always verify via Meta's /search?type=adlocale API before use. As of Sep 2026:
Russian: 17 · Ukrainian: 52 · English UK: 24 · English US: 6 · Spanish: 23 These IDs can change. Do not copy from this document into a campaign — always look them up fresh via the API. May 2026: locale 10 (labeled Russian) was actually Italian. 30 days, €141 wasted.
Age
25–54 Never widen above 54. The 55–64 age group generated clicks in early testing but zero bookings — the signal was noise. Stick to 25–54.
Detailed targeting
Leave interest targeting off for locale-based campaigns. Locale already narrows the audience. Adding interest targeting (ceramics, art, etc.) on top of locale in a small city creates an audience so narrow that the algorithm can't learn — you get inconsistent delivery and higher CPCs.
Advantage audience
OFF — set to {"advantage_audience": 0} If you leave this on, Meta can override your locale and age targeting and expand to whoever it thinks will click. That defeats the entire point of targeting Russian speakers in Valencia.
Placements
Instagram Feed + Stories + Reels. Remove Audience Network. Audience Network (third-party apps) burns budget on low-quality placements. Our audience is on Instagram. Facebook feed is secondary — keep it but watch the CPC split.

✓ Format that works
Short bold headline. One specific thing (what you'll make). Location signal. WhatsApp/DM CTA. Video of hands in clay if possible.
🏺 Learn ceramics in Valencia!

✗ Format that doesn't work
Emojis as decoration. Urgency language that isn't true. No specific hook. Generic CTA. Shows the class as a product, not an experience.
Creative element Recommendation Why
Format Video first, photo second Video autoplay stops the scroll. A photo of someone's hands in clay outperforms a flyer graphic every time.
Headline 1 specific thing. Under 6 words. "Make a mug this Thursday." Not "Join our exciting pottery experience!" Short and specific signals confidence.
Body copy 2–3 sentences max. Write like a person, not a flyer. Your audience is Russian/Ukrainian speakers who chose to live in Valencia. They're smart. Treat them that way.
CTA button Send Message or Learn More For our audience and price point (€40–€90), people want to ask a question before booking. "Send Message" opens WhatsApp/DM directly. Higher conversion than "Book Now."
Language Match the ad set language exactly Russian ad set → Russian copy. Ukrainian ad set → Ukrainian copy. Never run Russian copy to a Ukrainian locale ad set. Ever.
Images Real photos of real classes at Saged Stock ceramics photos are instantly recognised as stock. Real photos of your actual students convert better because they show the real community.
Size 1080×1080 (feed) + 1080×1920 (stories/reels) Always produce both. Stories and Reels are the highest-reach placements for this audience.

Incident log — mistakes that cost us money
Incident 1 · May 2026 · €141.89 lost

Locale bug — ads shown to Italians and Bulgarians

What happened: The Adult Ceramics campaign was set up with locale 10 (labeled in our notes as "Russian" — it's actually Italian) and locale 37 (labeled "Ukrainian" — it's actually Bulgarian). Russian-language ceramic workshop ads ran for 30 days to Italian and Bulgarian speakers in Valencia. Zero bookings.

Root cause: Locale IDs were copied from memory without verification.

Fix: Always query GET /search?type=adlocale&q=[language name] and confirm the returned name matches before use. Canonical IDs as of Sep 2026: RU=17, UA=52, EN-UK=24, EN-US=6, ES=23. But verify — don't trust this list either.

Incident 2 · Jul 2026 · €95.46 lost

Geo bug — ads shown to people in Egypt

What happened: All 7 active ad sets were built with city key 668776, which resolves to Shubra Tana, Egypt. We thought it was Valencia. Zero possible bookings from a correct audience.

Root cause: City key copied from an old document without verification.

Fix: Always query GET /search?type=adgeolocation&q=Valencia&location_types=["city"] and confirm name + country_code = ES before use. Correct Valencia key: 699854.

What actually drives bookings at Saged — honest attribution
Primary channel

Koritsia's Telegram community

Russian and Ukrainian-speaking residents of Valencia trust Koritsia personally. When she posts about a class in her community, people book. First booking in June 2026 came from a Telegram DM — not a Meta click. This channel is the studio's most effective acquisition tool and costs €0.

Secondary channel

Word of mouth

Students refer friends. Revenue in August–September 2026 held at break-even with zero paid ads. Satisfied students are the most efficient marketing channel in the studio. Every great class experience is a marketing event.

What this means for you as an instructor: Meta ads are a brand awareness layer, not the primary booking engine. Invest in making your classes genuinely great and shareable — photos, student experiences, community. That creates the word-of-mouth loop that actually fills seats. Use Meta to maintain visibility and reach people outside the existing community. Don't rely on it as your only acquisition channel.
