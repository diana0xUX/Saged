Nav School Options

Two pages share one nav shell: Saged ceramic studio (orange) vs Handpan school (purple). Three ways to make it clear which one you're on.

Option A
Colour dot on logo
A small dot appears under the logo in the school's accent colour. Everything else stays identical. The colour of the CTA pill already signals which school — the dot reinforces it.

Pro: No extra clutter. Pure and minimal. CTA button already does most of the work — this just adds a second hint.
Con: Very easy to miss. A first-time visitor reading fast won't see a 6px dot. Doesn't say "music" or "ceramics" in words.
Option B
School chip next to logo
A small badge reading "ШКОЛА ХЭНДПАНА" or "КЕРАМИКА" sits right next to the logo. Same coloured background as the CTA pill — consistent accent system.

Pro: Reads immediately — no guessing. Chip colour matches the CTA pill so the system is consistent. Easy to scan on mobile too.
Con: Adds one more element near the logo. On narrow screens the chip + long link list can start to crowd. Needs to hide or shrink on mobile.
Option C
Sub-label under logo name
The logo gains a small third line: "Школа хэндпана" in 10px muted colour. No badge, no chip — pure typography, same weight as the nav links.

Pro: Most typographically elegant. No extra UI elements. Works at any screen size — just text. The sub-label can even include the city ("Школа хэндпана · Валенсия").
Con: 10px is small — slightly harder to read on non-retina screens. The logo block becomes 3 lines tall which can feel crowded inside 62px height.
