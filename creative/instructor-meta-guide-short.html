Before you start
All campaigns go through Diana
Your ads run under the Saged Meta Business account. Send Diana your settings, creative, and copy for review before anything goes live. She activates all campaigns. Contact: WhatsApp +34 605 54 33 00 · Telegram @dvoroneca
What works at Saged — the short version
The studio's primary booking channels are Koritsia's Telegram community and word of mouth — not paid ads. Meta ads are a brand awareness layer that keeps Saged visible to new residents who later book through DM. Use them for visibility, not as your only booking channel. Your best ad is a great class that students want to talk about.

Step 2 — Ad sets (one per language)
One ad set per language — never merge RU and UA
Russian and Ukrainian audiences are separate communities. Create one ad set for Russian speakers, one for Ukrainian, one for English. Different copy for each. Never combine them into one ad set.
Ad set settings — same for every language
Ad set name
[Class] · RU · Valencia  /  [Class] · UA · Valencia  /  [Class] · EN · Valencia
Conversion location
Messaging apps (preferred) or Website Messaging opens a WhatsApp DM directly — higher conversion for this audience and price point than website checkout.
Budget
€3–€5 per day, or €50–€70 lifetime for a 2–3 week campaign
Schedule
Start 3 weeks before your class date. End date 2 days before.
Bid strategy
Lowest cost without cap

Step 4 — Creative
✓ Do this
·Video of hands in clay — even 15 seconds
·Headline: 1 specific thing, under 6 words
·Copy: 2–3 sentences, conversational tone
·CTA button: "Send Message" or "Learn More"
·Real photos of Saged classes — real students
·Language matches the ad set exactly (RU copy → RU ad set)
·Both 1:1 (feed) and 9:16 (stories) formats
✗ Avoid this
·Stock ceramic photos — audience can tell immediately
·Flyer-style text over image (prices, dates, logos)
·"Spaces filling fast!" urgency if it isn't true
·CTA "Book Now" or "Shop Now" — too transactional
·Emoji as decoration (one purposeful emoji max)
·Same creative for RU and UA — always separate
·Advertising events — organic only for events
Best performing format at Saged — English video
Short video (15–30s) showing hands working with clay. Clear single-line headline. WhatsApp CTA. In testing, this format achieved 5.17% CTR at €0.01 cost per click — reaching 29,000 people in 3 weeks on a small budget. Prioritise video over static image whenever possible.
Step 5 — Where to send people
Recommended — WhatsApp DM
Ad opens a WhatsApp conversation with Saged.

Pre-fill the message with the class name so Diana knows which class the person saw the ad for.

Why: For workshops at €40–€90, most people want to ask a question before paying. Message-first converts better than direct checkout.
Also works — Landing page
Send traffic to saged.club (RU) or saged.club/ua (UA). The booking section has both WhatsApp and Cal.com links.

Good for general awareness. The landing page has all the class information for people who want to read more before reaching out.
