## The Business

**Saged.club** is a ceramic and cultural workshop space in the old city of Valencia, Spain.

It is a real, physical studio — not a pop-up, not a course platform. People come to the address, make things with their hands, and leave with something they created. The studio also runs events: sound healing, breathwork, tea ceremonies, slow friending gatherings. The ceramics are the anchor; the events are the community layer around them.

The studio opened in 2026. It is owner-operated by Diana Sage (Diana Voroneca), a product designer and ceramicist who moved to Valencia and built this from scratch.

## Audience

### Primary: Russian and Ukrainian-speaking residents of Valencia
- Long-term residents, not tourists
- Women 25–45 predominantly
- Already connected through informal RU/UA community networks in Valencia
- Reach them through: Koritsia's personal Telegram network, word of mouth, Instagram
- Booking behaviour: DM-first ("message me, I'll send a link") — not click-to-checkout

### Secondary: English-speaking expats and long-term residents
- Internationals who haven't found their community activity yet
- No organic network to discover Saged — paid ads are the discovery channel for this group
- Same age range, similar psychographics
- Booking behaviour: same DM-first pattern
- Key insight from ad data: English-language ads outperformed RU/UA ads 5–6× in CTR (5.17% vs 0.77–0.88%) because the RU/UA audience already knows about Saged through community channels

### Not the audience
- Tourists
- Spanish-speaking locals (separate scene)
- Children without parents (kids classes require parent booking)

## Offerings

- Instructor: Koritsia — professional ceramic artist, teaches in Russian and Ukrainian
- Format: 2-day workshop (2 sessions × 2 hours)
- Schedule: Thursdays (2 classes) + Saturdays (2 classes) + Sundays (2 classes) = 6 weekend/weekday slots

## Brand Identity

### Personality
Warm · handmade · playful · community-rooted · honest

Not: corporate, minimalist-cold, tourist-facing, wellness-industry-polished

### Feeling to communicate
A real person's studio. Slightly imperfect. The piece you take home is lopsided and you love it more for that. A door slightly open, warm light inside, no pressure to enter.

### Voice
Direct, unafraid of its price, personal. "Hey — we do ceramics here on Thursdays. Koritsia teaches. It's €60. Come."

**Structure:**

## Known Brand Gaps (for audit)

1. **Colour inconsistency:** Two orange/terracotta values (`#FF7A23` ads vs `#9F4D2D` web) never reconciled.
2. **Font inconsistency:** Costa (primary brand font) not used on website — only in ads and print.
3. **No brand guidelines document:** Rules exist across multiple files (`creative/DESIGNER.md`, `CLAUDE.md`) but no single designed brand guide exists.
4. **Logo colour on dark:** Using CSS `filter: invert` to create white logo — not a proper white-on-dark asset.
5. **Ad creative gap:** No RU/UA ad creative for the adult ceramics class (only kids ads). Koritsia's 1080×1350 .ai file exists but the workflow for producing new sizes/variants isn't documented.
6. **Photo categorisation:** `koritsa-editorial.jpg` is a fashion editorial, not a studio photo — misleadingly labelled.
