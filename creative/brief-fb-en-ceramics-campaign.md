# Creative Brief — Facebook/Instagram Ad Campaign
## English Ceramics · Saged.club · September 2026

**For:** Visual designer
**Deliverable:** 3 ad format variants → WhatsApp inquiry
**Campaign context:** Paid Meta campaign targeting English-speaking expats in Valencia
**Brand reference:** Open `creative/brand-board.html` in Chrome before starting

---

## Why This Campaign Exists

English-speaking residents in Valencia have no organic way to discover Saged. The Russian and Ukrainian audience already knows about the studio through Koritsia's personal network. English-speakers don't. A paid ad is their only path.

This is not a brand awareness campaign. The only metric that matters is someone opening WhatsApp and writing: **"Hi, when is the next ceramics class?"**

The best-performing ad on record for this studio — an English-language video, "Saged.club is for..." — achieved CTR 5.17% and CPC €0.01 for three consecutive weeks. That outperformed Russian/Ukrainian variants by 5–6×. This campaign is the next iteration of that bet.

---

## The Audience

**English-speaking expats and long-term residents of Valencia, 25–45. Not tourists.**

They are looking for something to do that isn't another bar or another gym. They haven't found "their thing" in Valencia yet. Ceramics sounds interesting but they've never tried it. They're comfortable spending €60 on a real experience. When they see an ad, they DM — they don't click through to a booking page. They want to ask a question first.

What they feel before seeing the ad: mild FOMO about not having found their community activity yet.

What the ad should make them feel: "this is the obvious answer."

## Deliverables

Three formats. Design them as a set — same visual language, same creative direction, adapted to canvas.

### Format 1 — Instagram Feed 4:5 `(1080 × 1350 px)`
Primary format. Most screen real estate on mobile. This is the anchor — design this first, adapt the others from it.

A reference version already exists at `creative/ad-en-ceramics-v4.svg`. Use it as a starting point — the layout, colour logic, and hierarchy are right. The task is to bring better photography and tighter visual execution.

### Format 2 — Square Feed 1:1 `(1080 × 1080 px)`
Used for Facebook feed and Instagram feed on desktop. Compress the 4:5 layout: the photo takes the top half, the info block sits below. Price blob stays visible without scrolling.

### Format 3 — Stories / Reels 9:16 `(1080 × 1920 px)`
Full-screen vertical. Safe zones: top 250px (profile icon) and bottom 340px (CTA overlay) must be kept clear. Active design lives in the middle 66%. The story format can afford to be simpler — one big image, the essential text, the WhatsApp number. Don't try to fit everything from the feed ad.

## Visual Direction

**The reference:** `creative/library/05-ads/koritsia-ceramics/1080x1350-preview.jpg` — this is the Russian-language version that proved the design language works. Match its energy exactly.

**The feeling:** A door left slightly open. Warm light inside. Sounds of work. No pressure to enter. The piece you take home is lopsided and you love it more for that.

**What this looks like on the canvas:**
- Warm cream background (`#F9F4ED`) — never white, never grey
- Big confident headline in Costa font — two words max per line
- A real photo of someone working with clay — not a product shot, not stock
- Orange price blob, visible without scrolling (`#FF7A23`)
- WhatsApp number as the CTA — not "Book Now", not a website link
- 3–5 illustrated brand figures scattered at the edges at low opacity (0.45–0.55)
- Organic blob shapes, not geometric grids

**What this is NOT:**
- A spa or wellness aesthetic
- Minimalist-cold white space with tiny sans-serif
- Stock photography energy
- Banner-ad text layout (text block over blurred photo)

## Layout Rules

**3 focal points, nothing more.** Headline + photo + price blob. Everything else supports these three.

**Z-pattern:** Top-left "SAGED CLUB" label → logo top-right → diagonal across photo → price blob bottom-right. This is the natural eye path. Design to it.

**Squint test:** Half-close your eyes and look at the composition. The headline or the instructor's face must read first. If the figures or address block read first, the hierarchy is wrong.

**Price is always visible without scrolling.** Bottom half of the frame. Orange blob. If the viewer can't see €60 without scrolling or zooming, the layout fails.

**Figures at edges only.** The brand figures (illustrated characters from `creative/library/02-figures/`) frame the composition — they never compete with the content. 3–5 figures, edges and corners only, opacity 0.45–0.55.
