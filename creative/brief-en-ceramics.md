# Creative Brief — English Ceramics Ad
**Format:** Instagram feed 4:5 (1080×1350)
**Deliverable:** Static image ad → WhatsApp inquiry
**Current version:** `ad-en-ceramics-v2.svg`

---

## Purpose

Drive WhatsApp messages from English-speaking residents of Valencia who want to try a pottery workshop but have no community connector to find it organically.

This ad fills a specific gap: the RU/UA audience already knows about Saged through Koritsia's personal network — Telegram, word of mouth, her teaching community. English-speakers have no equivalent path. For them, a paid ad is the only discovery channel that exists. The purpose is not brand awareness — it's generating one specific action: someone opening WhatsApp and asking "when is the next class?"

---

## Audience

**Primary:** English-speaking expats and long-term residents of Valencia, 25–45. Not tourists.

They are:
- Living in Valencia (home location targeting, 17km radius around city centre)
- Looking for something to do that isn't a bar or a gym
- Curious about making things with their hands but have never done it
- Comfortable spending €60 on an experience if it feels personal and real
- More likely to DM than to click through to a booking page — they want to ask a question first

They are not:
- Tourists (wrong geo targeting, wrong life stage)
- People who already do ceramics (too advanced, different need)
- Spanish speakers (they have their own local ceramics scene)

**What they feel before seeing the ad:** mild FOMO about not having found "their thing" in Valencia yet. The ad should make ceramics feel like the obvious answer to that.

---

## Positioning

**The only English-language pottery workshop in Valencia's old city.**

Not the cheapest, not the biggest. The most personal. Six people maximum means Koritsia knows your name by the end of the first session.

Competitors: large ceramics schools (impersonal, Spanish-only), tourist activity providers (expensive, tourist-facing), DIY pottery studios (no instruction).

Saged sits in the gap: small enough to feel like a friend's studio, professional enough that you actually learn.

**Single most important thing the ad must communicate:** "This is a real class, in English, in Valencia, taught by a real artist, and it costs €60."

Everything else is detail.

---

## Personality

Warm. Handmade. A little playful. Honest.

Not: polished brand-agency cool, minimalist wellness aesthetic, tourist-brochure cheerfulness.

The reference is the Koritsia RU/UA ad (`library/05-ads/koritsia-ceramics/1080x1350-preview.jpg`) — that energy is right. Organic blob shapes, brand figures scattered at the edges, Costa font big and confident, a real photo of a real person, price visible and unashamed.

If the ad could have a voice it would say: "Hey — we do ceramics here on Thursdays. Koritsia teaches. It's €60. Come."

---

## Feeling

The feeling of making something with your hands for the first time and being surprised that it works.

Not perfect. Not Instagram-ready. Real clay, real effort, real person standing next to you correcting your grip. The piece you take home is slightly lopsided and you love it more for that.

The ad should feel like a door left slightly open — warm light inside, sounds of work, no pressure to enter.
