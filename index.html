How it works
01
Message us
WhatsApp or Instagram — we confirm your slot and answer any questions the same day. No booking form, no waiting.
02
Come for two sessions
Shape your piece by hand in session one. Return a week later to glaze and decorate. No experience needed — materials included.
03
Take it home
We handle both firings. About two weeks after your second session, your finished ceramic is ready to collect from the studio.
