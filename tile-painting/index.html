About the workshop

Step into our welcoming ceramics studio and turn a ceramic tile into something completely yours. Discover Spanish tile designs, choose your colours and patterns, and paint with Ekaterina's guidance. Enjoy two relaxed hours of creativity, drinks, light bites, and good company.
