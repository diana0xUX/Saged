Last updated: 2026-05-11

## Why pinned posts matter (the analytical case)

On Instagram, the **first thing a visitor sees** above the post grid is your bio + the pinned posts (up to 3). When someone arrives via an ad, a hashtag, a friend's tag, or a Google search — they spend 2-3 seconds on the bio before deciding to scroll or leave.

A pinned workshop post does four things:
1. **Removes the question "what is this?"** — even if your last 30 posts are sound-meditation, candle-care, and DM stories, the pinned post says "we run ceramics every Thursday."
2. **Compresses the funnel** — the CTA is right there. No need to dig through old posts.
3. **Survives the algorithm** — new content gets reshuffled; pinned content stays.
4. **Cross-account discovery** — when @korytsia_studio's pinned post says "I teach at @saged.club," that's a free traffic loop between the two accounts.

For a studio at this scale (no website yet on the custom domain, manual DM booking), **the bio + pinned post is your landing page**. Treat them as design surfaces, not afterthoughts.
