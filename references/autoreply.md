# Autoreply pack — Saged.club

For Meta Business Suite (Instagram + Messenger) and WhatsApp Business app. Three things:

1. **Greeting / Instant Reply** — sent the first time someone DMs you
2. **Away Message** — sent when you're offline (set hours: e.g., 22:00–10:00)
3. **Quick Reply FAQs** — taps/keywords that pull up canned answers

Voice = warm, brief, in-language. No corporate. We always close the loop with a human within hours.

## Where to paste this

### Instagram (via Meta Business Suite app or web)
1. Open **Meta Business Suite** → **Inbox** → **Automations** (left sidebar)
2. Click **+ Create automation** OR find the existing **Instant Reply** + **Away Message**
3. Paste the greeting → Save
4. Repeat for Away Message
5. For FAQs: Inbox → Saved Replies → + New saved reply

### WhatsApp Business (mobile app)
1. Open WhatsApp Business app → **Business tools** (top right menu)
2. **Greeting message** → ON → paste the greeting → Save
3. **Away message** → ON → paste away → set schedule 22:00–10:00 → Save
4. **Quick replies** → + New → paste each FAQ with its `/keyword` shortcut

### Facebook Messenger (via Business Suite)
Same as Instagram — Instant Reply, Away Message, FAQ are shared across both platforms in Business Suite.

## Tuning the autoreply later

After a week of seeing real DMs:
- If 70%+ of people ask the same thing → bake it into the greeting
- If parents ask very different questions than adults → consider sending them to dedicated landings (`saged.club/kids/`)
- Track which `/quick` reply gets used most — that's your highest-volume FAQ
- If away message triggers a lot of "ok thanks bye" replies → tighten the hours window
