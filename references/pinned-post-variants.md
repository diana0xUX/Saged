# How to pin

**Instagram (@saged.club + @korytsia_studio):**

1. Post the chosen variant as a new feed post — use the **hero-ru.mp4** or **hero-ua.mp4** video as the visual (or a strong product photo)
2. Once posted, **long-press the post** → **Pin to your profile**
3. You can pin up to **3 posts** — consider pinning the workshop + a "Saged.club is for…" identity post + a process video

**Why a video over a photo for the pin:** the hero videos auto-play silently on the profile preview when someone visits. That movement catches the eye in a way a static photo doesn't. Use a strong process clip (shaping clay) for max stop-scroll power.

# A/B note

If you have bandwidth to test:
- Week 1: pin Variant A (process-led) — measure profile visits → DMs
- Week 2: swap to Variant B (identity-led) — measure same
- Pick whichever drives more DMs, keep pinned indefinitely

If no bandwidth: **just pick Variant A** and ship. Don't optimise before the floor is built.
