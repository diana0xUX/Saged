What's included

Your own workspace in the studio. One block of white clay. Clear glaze. Access to tools and equipment.

When

Daily 10:00 – 22:00, in the free time between masterclasses. Usually busy on Thursdays and Saturdays. We'll send the calendar when you join.

Charged separately

Firing — €6 per shelf.

You can teach in the studio. You reimburse material costs (clay + glaze), and revenue is split 50/50 with the studio.
