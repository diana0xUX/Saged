What the kids do

Day 1–2

Meet the clay. Hand-build a piece — a cup, a small bowl, a figurine, whatever they want.

Day 3

Glaze + color. They choose how to decorate their work. The studio handles the second firing.

Day 4

Final assembly, photo with their finished piece. Kids take their ceramics home.

Common questions

What should we bring?

Nothing — we provide everything. Just dress your kid in clothes that can get a bit dirty. Aprons are at the studio.

Does my child need any experience?

None. This camp is designed for kids touching clay for the first time. Korytsia works with each child individually.

Is it safe with the kiln and firing?

Yes. Firings are done by the studio — kids only participate in hand-building and decoration. They don't go near the kiln.

Can I register two kids from the same family?

Of course. Message us and we'll discuss the age range and group composition.

When does my kid bring their ceramics home?

At the end of camp (Day 4). By then all pieces have been fired twice.

What if my child changes their mind halfway?

It happens. If something's not working — message us, we'll figure it out together. We know camp is an experiment for many families.

What language is the camp in?

Korytsia teaches in Russian and Ukrainian comfortably. English-speaking kids welcome — basic instructions translate fine, and kids communicate through the work itself.
