What happens in class

Meet the clay

Korytsia shows how clay behaves in your hands. Kids try shapes, decide what they want to make.

Build a piece

Each kid makes their own — a small bowl, a cup, a figurine, whatever they want. Korytsia helps each child individually.

Color + decorate

At the end of class, kids choose a glaze. The studio handles both firings afterward.

Finished ceramics ready ~2 weeks after class — pick up from the studio or we deliver in Valencia.

Common questions

What should we bring?

Nothing — we provide everything. Just dress your kid in clothes that can get a bit dirty. Aprons at the studio.

Does my child need experience?

None. The class is designed for first-timers. Korytsia helps each child individually.

Is it safe with the kiln?

Yes. Firings happen later, done by the studio — kids only participate in hand-building and decoration. No kid goes near the kiln.

Can I register two kids from the same family?

Yes. Message us, we'll discuss ages and group fit.

What if my child doesn't enjoy it?

It happens. This is a trial class — that's exactly the point. If it doesn't click, that's fine, no obligation to continue.

When does my kid get their finished ceramics?

About 2 weeks after class. Pick up from the studio or we deliver in Valencia.

How do I pay?

Once a class date is set, we send a Stripe payment link. Spot is confirmed once paid.

What language is the class in?

Korytsia teaches in Russian and Ukrainian comfortably. English-speaking kids welcome — basic instructions translate easily.
