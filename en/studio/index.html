Session 1 — Shape

Two hours with clay. Build your piece by hand — a bowl, a vase, a mug, beads, whatever you want. Koritsia guides every step.

Session 2 — Glaze

A week later, your piece comes back from the first firing. You choose colours, apply glaze, decorate. The studio does the final firing.

What you take home

Your piece — fired, glazed, finished. Ready to live with you. Two weeks after the second session.

Your instructor

Koritsia / Корица

A Ukrainian ceramic artist based in Valencia, Koritsia works as the ceramic resident at Saged.club. For her, clay is a slow, tactile material — it doesn't tolerate rushing, and that's the point.

She hand-builds ceramics of every kind — bowls, vases, candleholders, beads, small sculptures. Each course is two sessions with a week in between, because the clay needs time: it must dry and go through its first firing before you can glaze it. Groups stay small — up to 6 — so she knows your name by the end of the first session.

Koritsia teaches in English, Ukrainian, and Russian. Sessions happen in whatever language the group prefers.

Questions

Do I need experience?

None. We start from zero and go at your pace. Koritsia helps everyone.

Why two sessions, not one?

The clay needs a week to dry and go through the first firing before you can glaze it. That's just how ceramics works — we don't rush it.

Can I start any Thursday?

Yes. The course runs every Thursday. Book two consecutive Thursdays (e.g. the 11th + 18th September) in whichever time slot works for you. Just message us your preferred date.

What should I wear?

Anything you don't mind getting dirty. Clay washes out but can leave marks. Aprons are provided.

What if I miss the second session?

The studio can glaze for you — but it's worth coming. Choosing the colour and decoration is the best part. You can also reschedule your second session to the following Thursday.

When do I get my piece?

Two weeks after your second session. Pick it up at the studio, or we can arrange delivery within Valencia.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes — book two spots on the same Thursday and let us know. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm both together.

Is there a gift certificate?

Yes. Message or email us and we'll send a named certificate within the day. See gift options →

Is it really taught in English?

Yes. Koritsia teaches in English, Ukrainian, and Russian. The session runs in whatever language the group prefers — but if you book and you're the only English speaker, it'll be in English.
