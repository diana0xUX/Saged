Meet the team

Teacher · Musician · Russian / English

Denis Balykin

Denis has been playing and teaching handpan for 10 years. His classes are calm and intuitive, centred on feeling, rhythm and finding your own musical voice. No music theory needed, just curiosity. All levels welcome.

Instrument maker · Handcrafted

Eugene Platonov

Eugene makes handpans by hand, including the instruments you’ll play in class. Each one is carefully crafted and tuned to be felt as much as heard.
